🚀 Ready to Publish on Netlify Drop?
  1. Create a folder named birthday-book with this index.html, style.css, script.js and an assets/ subfolder containing song.mp3 and your photos.
  2. Open script.js in any text editor and update BOOK_CONFIG at the top with your paths, lyrics, and exact timestamps (in seconds).
  3. Drag the entire birthday-book folder onto app.netlify.com/drop to get your shareable link!
